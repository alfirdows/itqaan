# Itqaan · إتقان

A calm, ADHD-friendly way to learn Quran tajweed (Ḥafṣ ʿan ʿĀṣim) from zero to mastery, in Arabic or English.

**Open the site:** open `index.html` in any browser. It is one self-contained file with no build step.

## What's inside

- **Learn:** 5 levels and 17 short lessons, about 3 minutes each.
  1. 🌱 Start from zero: letters, vowel marks, what tajweed is, Istiʿādhah & Basmalah, hamzat al-waṣl
  2. 🌿 Core rules: noon sākinah & tanwīn, meem sākinah, ghunnah & qalqalah, lām rules, madd
  3. 🌳 How letters sound: makhārij, ṣifāt, tafkhīm & tarqīq
  4. ⛰️ Advanced: mithlayn / mutajānisayn / mutaqāribayn, waqf & ibtidāʾ (with the 4 saktāt), special Ḥafṣ words
  5. 🏆 Mastery exam
- **One idea per screen:** a big word with colored letters, a picture of the rule, then a "tap the letter" challenge.
- **No pressure:** a 🌿 break every ~2 minutes, your place is saved, kind feedback, stars, and a days-learned count that never resets.
- **Tap to explain:** tap underlined terms for a simple explanation, tap a verse for its meaning, or tap "Explain like I'm 5".
- **Practice, Test, Review:** questions only come from lessons you've finished, and mistakes come back until you get them right twice.
- **عربي / English** toggle with full right-to-left support.
